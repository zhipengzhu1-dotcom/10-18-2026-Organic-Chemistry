01Paper & ink

White paper, near-black pen, six marker colours. Pen inks are text-safe on paper (contrast shown live). Marker fills are too pale to stand alone on white, so a fill always sits inside a pen line, and text on a fill is always pen ink. No red anywhere: "not quite" is pink.

02Type

Four faces, each with one job. The script face is a signature, not a typeface: the wordmark and one hero word, 40px and up. Handwriting for headings and labels; a plain rounded sans for anything a Student reads closely; mono for formulas and numbers.

04Motion: drawn on twos

Everything moves at 20 fps, like a flip-book. We keep Emil's rules (ease-out on entry, under 300ms for UI, press feedback right away, exit faster than entry, nothing loops) and sample the ease-out curve once per frame, holding each drawing. The first frame lands at once, so a press still answers instantly. Pick a frame rate in the top bar to feel the difference.

    05A Scene, stop-motion

    Scenes stay pure functions of t. The only change is the clock: the player snaps t to the frame grid before handing it over, and while a Scene plays, its pen lines boil (one of three wobble seeds per frame). When it ends, the boil stops. Nothing loops. Under reduced motion it opens paused on its poster frame, with no boil.
