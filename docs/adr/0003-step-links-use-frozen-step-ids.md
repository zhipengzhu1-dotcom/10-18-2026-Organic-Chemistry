# Step links use frozen Step ids, not Step positions

A link to a Step ends in its Step id (`/organic/orgo-1/structure-bonding/resonance-pushing-electrons#s07`), not its position (`#step-7`), because videos on the future channel will point Viewers at exact Steps and a published video can't be edited when a Lesson changes. Step ids freeze once the site is public. A new Step takes the next unused id even when it sits mid-Lesson, ids are never reused, and a deleted Step's id stays in the Lesson file pointing at the Step its old links now open. So "Step 7 of 15" on screen may be `s09` in the URL, and that's deliberate. We chose this over position numbers plus a redirect table, which would need a manual mapping every time a Lesson is edited (see "Decide the video-ready hooks", #51).
